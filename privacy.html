Privacy Policy

PhotoPick for iPhone and iPad · Last updated 25 September 2026

PhotoPick does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services. Your photos stay on your device and in your own Apple Photos library.

Your photos

  • PhotoPick reads the Photos metadata you allow it to access to show a visual browser of photos and albums. You choose individual photos, a range or all photos in the current source before review starts. Apple’s system picker is also available. When you ask to add recent photos to an album, it uses capture dates in the Photos library you have allowed it to access and shows you the proposed additions first. Nothing is added until you confirm. It records your choices — keep, remove, unsure — review settings, any explicit screenshot exclusions, and the order you arrange your keepers in. Skipping screenshots does not delete them.
  • Full originals are not copied by default. The app refers to them in your Apple Photos library. If a photo is stored in iCloud, iOS may download it from your own iCloud account so it can be shown to you. PhotoPick keeps bounded, disposable display previews on your device to make browsing faster. They are not used instead of originals when saving or sharing.
  • When you choose Share photos, you select which kept photos to send through the iOS share sheet. PhotoPick prepares temporary original files for that action and removes them after sharing finishes. The receiving app or service handles the photos under its own privacy policy.
  • You may optionally download copies of an album for offline review. Those copies are stored only on your device and can be deleted from within the app.
  • "Remove" excludes a photo from your album. It does not delete anything. Deleting photos from Apple Photos is a separate action that asks you to confirm the exact photos first.
  • Nothing is uploaded to us. We operate no servers and receive no photographs.

Sharing photos with people nearby

  • PhotoPick can share original photos directly with other people's devices over a local Wi-Fi connection, but only when you choose to start or join a shared album.
  • These transfers are encrypted and go directly between devices. They do not pass through any server of ours, and no copy is retained by us.
  • Your own keep/remove choices are never transmitted. Only the photographs you explicitly contribute are shared.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or email collection.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting services, or advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data sold or shared with anyone.

Diagnostics

The app writes basic diagnostic messages to the iOS system log on your device — counts and timings, such as how long an import took. These never include photographs, filenames, or locations, and they never leave your device unless you choose to send Apple a diagnostic report.

Permissions

  • Photos — to browse allowed photos and albums, show your selected photos, find recent photos to add when you ask, and save your finished album back to your library. Limited access shows only the photos you have allowed.
  • Local network — only for sharing photos with people nearby.
  • Camera — only if you scan a QR code to join a shared album.

Each is requested at the moment it is needed, and the app works without granting the last two.

Children

PhotoPick collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.
