Privacy Policy

PhotoPick for iPhone and iPad · Last updated 24 October 2026

There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your photos stay on your device and in your own Apple Photos library, unless you add them to a shared album with friends. Over the internet, they are encrypted on your phone first, so we cannot see them.

Your photos

  • PhotoPick reads the Photos metadata you allow it to access to show a visual browser of photos and albums. You choose individual photos, a range or all photos in the current source before review starts. When you ask to add recent photos to an album, it uses capture dates in the Photos library you have allowed it to access and shows you the proposed additions first. Nothing is added until you confirm. It records your choices — keep or remove — review settings, any explicit screenshot exclusions, and the order you arrange your keepers in. Skipping screenshots does not delete them.
  • With Photos access already granted, Home can use capture dates to show “On this day” photos from earlier years. This lookup and its small display previews stay on your device. It respects limited access and excludes hidden photos. You explicitly choose which photos to review; discovery never chooses favourites or creates an album automatically.
  • Full originals are not copied by default. The app refers to them in your Apple Photos library. If a photo is stored in iCloud, iOS may download it from your own iCloud account so it can be shown to you. PhotoPick keeps bounded, disposable display previews on your device to make browsing faster. They are not used instead of originals when saving or sharing.
  • When you choose Share photos, you select which kept photos to send through the iOS share sheet. PhotoPick prepares a temporary full-quality copy of each, as it looks in Photos with your edits, starting as you select them when you are on Wi-Fi, and removes these copies when you leave the sharing screen. Sharing one photo from Swipe works the same way, for that photo alone. The receiving app or service handles the photos under its own privacy policy.
  • You may optionally download copies of an album for offline review. Those copies are stored only on your device and can be deleted from within the app.
  • "Remove" excludes a photo from your album. It does not delete anything. Deleting photos from Apple Photos is a separate action that asks you to confirm the exact photos first.
  • Edit can crop, rotate or flip a photo. Only when you tap Done does PhotoPick change that photo in your Apple Photos library, on your device, without making a copy. Apple Photos keeps the original: Revert to Original, in PhotoPick or Apple Photos, brings it back.
  • Photo details shows where a photo was taken on an Apple Maps map, with the place’s name. Opening details on a photo with a location sends that location to Apple for the map and the name; nothing reaches us.
  • Nothing is uploaded to us unless you add photos to a shared album over the internet, below.

Sharing photos with people nearby

  • PhotoPick can share original photos directly with other people's devices over a local Wi-Fi connection, but only when you choose to start or join a shared album.
  • These transfers are encrypted and go directly between devices. They do not pass through any server of ours, and no copy is retained by us.
  • Your own keep/remove choices are never transmitted. Only the photographs you explicitly contribute are shared.
  • Deleting a shared album removes its local room record and invitation credentials. Downloaded photos stay on your device, accessible in Settings → Saved photos until you review them into a personal album or explicitly delete those saved photos. Deleting a room does not delete photos from Apple Photos or from friends’ devices.
  • Shared photos are originals, with their embedded details, such as where and when they were taken. Everyone in the album can see them.

Shared albums over the internet

  • When you add photos to a shared album over the internet, they go through our server, run by Cloudflare, to the album’s other members. Your phone encrypts each photo, its previews and its embedded details before it leaves, along with the album’s name and everyone’s names. The key travels only inside the invitation link, so we cannot open them.
  • Anyone with the invitation link can join and see the album’s photos, their details (where they were taken, too) and the name each person chose.
  • What our server can read: random IDs for the album, its members and their phones; dates and limits; who added each photo and when; file sizes; approvals; which files each phone downloaded, which can hint at photos you kept but not what they show; and for each phone, a push token, an Apple App Attest key, whether it wants notifications, until when it muted the album and the members it blocked. Your keep/remove choices, your albums and your Photos library never leave your phone.
  • Our server sees your internet address with each request, as any website does, to limit abuse. It does not store it.
  • When friends add photos, or photos wait for your approval, our server asks Apple to show you a notification. It carries random IDs for the album, the person and a photo, and how many photos; the album’s and the person’s names travel encrypted, and your phone opens them to write the message. Apple and we see only “New photos in a shared album”. Other changes send a silent notification naming only the album’s ID. While PhotoPick is open, it keeps a connection to our server that only says an album changed. App Attest checks that requests come from the real app.
  • People who join in a web browser use the same encryption, done by the page. The page’s code comes from our server each time, so that promise also rests on us serving honest code.
  • An album’s photos stay on the server for 30 days after the last one was added, and never more than 90 days after the first. An album without photos ends after 90 days, and its host can end it sooner. Then its files and records are deleted within a day; Cloudflare’s database keeps a restorable history for 30 more days. Photos already downloaded stay on members’ phones.
  • Reports: if you report a photo, your phone sends us that photo readable so we can look at it. It is the only time we see a photo. A report is kept until we deal with it, then deleted, with its photo, 90 days later.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or email collection. Settings → Feedback only opens a message in your own mail app, addressed to me, with the app version, device model and iOS version written into it where you can see and delete them. Nothing is sent unless you send it, and PhotoPick itself sends nothing.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting services, or advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data sold or shared with anyone.

Diagnostics

The app writes basic diagnostic messages to the iOS system log on your device — counts and timings, such as how long an import took. These never include photographs, filenames, or locations, and they never leave your device unless you choose to send Apple a diagnostic report.

Permissions

  • Photos — to browse allowed photos and albums, show your selected photos, find recent photos to add when you ask, rediscover photos from this day in past years, save your finished album back to your library, save friends’ photos you keep from a shared album to your library, and save crops, rotations and flips you make with Edit to that photo. Limited access shows only the photos you have allowed.
  • Local network — only for sharing photos with people nearby.
  • Camera — only if you scan a QR code to join a shared album.
  • Notifications — for a reminder on the last evening of each month to make that month’s album and, in shared albums, when friends add photos or the album’s photos are about to leave the server. Your phone writes the reminders itself; friends’ photos come through Apple from our server, as above. Turn each on or off in Settings → Notifications, or mute one album in its details.

Notifications are asked for only if you say yes to the monthly reminder or to friends’ photos, or from Settings → Notifications. The others are requested at the moment they are needed. The app works without granting the last three.

Children

PhotoPick is not directed at children and asks no one’s age. Shared albums are for people aged 13 and over, or older where local law requires it (Terms of Use). Apart from photos sent in a report, we cannot read anyone’s photos or names.
