Community Rules

Shared albums are for the photos of an occasion: a birthday, a weekend, a wedding, a night out. Treat other people’s photos and faces as you’d want yours treated.

Never share, in any shared album:

  1. Anything that sexualises a child. Zero tolerance: we remove the person and report them to the police.
  2. Intimate or sexual images of someone without their consent, or threats to share them.
  3. Pornography or sexually explicit content.
  4. Anything selling or offering drugs, weapons or other illegal goods or services.
  5. Threats, incitement to violence, or praise of terrorism.
  6. Harassment, bullying or hate against people for who they are.
  7. Photos taken secretly in private places (bathrooms, changing rooms, bedrooms), or photos shared to expose someone’s private information.
  8. Other people’s work you have no right to share.
  9. Malware, spam, or attempts to break into albums, the server or other people’s phones.

Don’t make false or abusive reports.

If you’re the host, invite people you’re happy to be responsible for. Keep the invitation off public posts, and remove what breaks these rules.

What happens: a photo can be removed, a person removed, an album ended, the phone that made an album barred from making more, and illegal content reported to the police.

If someone is in immediate danger, call the emergency number first: 112 in Europe, 911 in the US.

Child safety standard

PhotoPick has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation.

  • Reporting: in any album, report the photo or the person with A child may be at risk, or write to edoardo.vaira@icloud.com. Never send us the material itself by email, and don’t save or forward it.
  • What we do:
    1. We keep the reported photo on our server, restricted, and stop its normal deletion.
    2. We remove the person and their photos, and end the album if it was made for this.
    3. If they made the album, we stop their phone from making more.
    4. We report to the Polizia Postale (Italy’s national centre against online child abuse, CNCPO) and to NCMEC in the United States, and give them what we hold.
  • Prevention: albums are invitation-only, there’s no way to search for albums or people, there are no private messages, and every album ends.
  • Contact for child-safety matters: edoardo.vaira@icloud.com.
