Common questions

Where can I learn how to use PhotoPick?
The updated introduction appears once, including for existing users. You can skip it; your albums and choices stay as they are. Open Settings → What PhotoPick is to see how to choose photos, review them and save your album. How to review photos explains the controls. Take a practice round lets you try sample photos without choosing your own. Practice is optional and resets when you close it.
How do I select a whole album or a range of photos?
When creating an album in the updated selection screen, choose Photos or Albums. Select all includes the photos in the view you are browsing. Select a range lets you tap a first and last photo and includes everything between them; you can adjust individual photos before tapping Review. The album gets a name automatically, which you can change during review or on the finished album. Apple’s picker is also available from the selection menu. Limited access shows only allowed photos. If a selected photo becomes unavailable, PhotoPick keeps your selection so you can retry or go back and adjust it; it does not silently leave photos out.
How can I browse a large photo library faster?
Pinch the gallery to make thumbnails smaller or larger. You can also choose Larger thumbnails or Smaller thumbnails in the ••• menu. Drag the slim date control on the right to move through a long library. Your selection stays selected while you change the view.
Do gallery thumbnails need to download?
The gallery uses available local Photos thumbnails first and prepares nearby rows as you scroll. A small preview stays visible even if a sharper version cannot load. Larger thumbnails request more detail; an internet connection may be needed if Photos has only a small preview or no local image. The cloud button retries a missing thumbnail without selecting it. Thumbnail size does not change the quality of saved originals.
Can I change the order or skip screenshots while reviewing?
Open the ••• menu to switch between By date and Shuffled without losing your choices or moving the current photo. Skip remaining screenshots excludes only screenshots you have not decided on yet. It does not delete them or mark them removed. Screenshots you already kept, removed or left unsure stay as you chose. Restore brings skipped screenshots back, including after finishing an album. You can also open the separate, confirmed deletion screen from review to delete photos you already marked Remove, without finishing the entire library first.
Does "Remove" delete my photo?
No. Remove excludes a photo from the album you're making. Your photo stays in Apple Photos exactly as it was. Deleting from your library is a separate screen that shows you the exact photos first and asks you to confirm.
Will I lose my progress if I close the app?
No. Your choices save as you make them. Close the app whenever you like and pick up where you left off. The sample practice round resets when closed.
Why are some photos slow to appear?
If your photos live in iCloud rather than on the device, iOS downloads them as you reach them. You can prepare a collection for offline review in advance from the album's menu, or tap its download icon in Settings → App storage.
Photos stay blank while Low Power Mode is on. What can I try?
Try turning Low Power Mode off in Settings → Battery, return to PhotoPick and retry the photo. Apple can pause iCloud Photos syncing to save battery. Check your connection and try opening the same photo in Apple Photos too. You do not need to turn off iCloud Photos or delete your collection. Apple's guide to paused Photos syncing explains how to check its status.
Can I add more photos to an existing album?
In builds with Add photos, open it from your album or the review menu. Choose photos lets you pick any photos. Add recent photos finds photos taken since the latest capture date already in that album, excluding photos it already contains. Check the preview, uncheck anything that belongs elsewhere, and confirm. Earlier choices and the cover stay saved; new photos start undecided. Save album to Photos again after reviewing them to update the same exported album. Older photos received later may fall outside the recent range: use Choose photos for those. With limited Photos access, only allowed photos can be added. This action is for your personal albums; nearby shared collections receive photos through their existing contribution flow.
Does saving an album reduce my photo quality?
No. Saving to Apple Photos reuses your original photos — nothing is re-encoded or duplicated. The album simply points at the photos you already have.
How does sharing with friends work?
Over local Wi-Fi, directly between devices, with both apps open. Nothing goes through a server. Everyone keeps their own private choices — your picks never affect anyone else's.

Reporting a problem

The most useful report says what you expected, what happened instead, and how many photos were involved. Screenshots or a screen recording are ideal. You will not be asked to send any of your photographs.
